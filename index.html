Read SAP reports

Capture a report you have open in Chrome or Edge. Captures go to a preview; you confirm the period and completeness before importing. Stock, settings and availability are never guessed.

Install the read-only capture helper
  1. Update your SailERP checkout. Open Chrome's chrome://extensions or Edge's edge://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the SailERP/extension folder.
  3. Keep SailERP open. In the SAP report tab, open SailERP capture, click Capture visible report, then Send to SailERP preview.
  4. Optionally start watching that report for rendered-data changes. The helper does not refresh SAP or navigate between reports.

Sales imports support Material, Quantity, Net Value and total Cost. Price Market and Production reports need manual interpretation. Empty reports stay missing.
